# SafeStep

**AI-powered digital safety companion for understanding suspicious online content.**

SafeStep helps people, especially older adults and less technical users, understand potentially suspicious screenshots, messages, emails, and online content through explainable AI analysis, deterministic risk scoring, clear guidance, and reassurance.

The goal is not simply to tell users whether something is "safe" or "unsafe." SafeStep is designed to help users understand **what they are looking at, why it may be suspicious, what they should do next, and how concerned they should be.**

## Problem

Online scams are becoming increasingly difficult to recognize.

Suspicious content can appear as:

- Text messages
- Emails
- Login pages
- Social media messages
- Fake delivery notifications
- Banking alerts
- Password-reset requests
- Other misleading online content

For someone who is not highly technical, the hardest question is often not:

> "Is this a scam?"

It is:

> **"What exactly is happening here, and what should I do?"**

SafeStep focuses on answering that question.
